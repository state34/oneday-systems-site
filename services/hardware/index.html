Hardware

Hardware sourcing and installation

Hardware chosen for the work

One Day Systems is a technology consulting firm serving the Kansas City metro that selects, sources, installs, and supports technology hardware for schools, businesses, government, and industrial sites.

The problem

Gear gets bought in a hurry, quoted into a stall, or left unsupported six months later. The wrong display, a switch that cannot carry the room, laptops that never joined the standard. Good design dies in the purchase.

How we work

Evaluate

What you already own, what is failing the work, and what a quote is pretending to solve.

Design

A standard: the devices that fit the rooms, the desks, and the network — specific enough to reorder.

Integrate

Installed so the hardware belongs to the system. Labeled, recorded, and talking to the rest of it.

Deploy

In service, with support that already knows what is on the wall. Not a pile of boxes and a goodbye.

Typical outcomes

  • Devices that fit the work.
  • A record of what you own, and a standard you can buy again.
  • Support from people who already know the design.

Questions

Do we have to buy the equipment from you?

No. We can procure equipment through One Day Systems, through the partners you already use, or point you to the best place to buy it. The job is the right fit, not a SKU.

Can you install hardware we already own?

Yes. Our team, your team, or a trusted partner can handle implementation. We plan the work so the handoff is clean whichever way you go.

Which brands do you install?

We select against the design. We are not locked to one manufacturer.

Do you serve both Kansas and Missouri?

Yes. One Day Systems serves the Kansas City metro on both sides of the state line.

How fast can you start?

Often within days of scope agreement. Multi-site school work follows your calendar, including summers and breaks when that is what the buildings need.

What happens after deployment?

Documentation, credentials in your name, training for the people who use it, and a clear handoff.

One Day Systems takes on the tech you keep putting off until “one day.”
