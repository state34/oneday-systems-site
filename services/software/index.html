Software that retires the workaround

Custom tools, integrations, and automation.

The problem

A spreadsheet is doing a job a product should do. People retype between systems. The internal tool was scoped once, then life happened, and it joined the list called “one day.”

How we work

Evaluate

The work, not only the feature request. Where time actually goes, and which swivel-chair step should not exist.

Design

A tool, an integration, or an automation the people who live in it will recognize. Small enough to finish. Clear enough to explain.

Integrate

Connected to the systems you already run, so the new piece removes a step instead of adding a login.

Deploy

In people’s hands, with a handover. Not a repository and a hope.

Typical outcomes

  • Custom tools that earn a place in the week.
  • Integrations that retire the retyping.
  • Automation you can explain to the next person.
