IT consulting and network projects in Kansas City

IT that people can rely on

One Day Systems is a technology consulting firm serving the Kansas City metro that evaluates, designs, integrates and deploys IT and network projects for schools and businesses.

The problem

The stack grew by exception. A network refresh has a deck and no owner. Endpoints carry workarounds. Cloud is a second system nobody drew on the same page. People keep working. The project stays on the list.

How we work

Evaluate

What is actually running, who is routing around it, and which refresh has been waiting on a quarter that never arrives.

Design

A network, endpoint, and cloud standard the people who support it can defend — not a catalog of everything a vendor can sell.

Integrate

Identity, access, and the tools already in use, connected on purpose so the new piece does not become another island.

Deploy

Staged so the week continues. Documented so support still makes sense after the project team steps back.

Typical outcomes

  • A network and endpoint baseline the company trusts.
  • Cloud that belongs to the system, not a parallel mystery.
  • Support that sticks — named, repeatable, and still useful next quarter.

Questions

What does an evaluation include?

A clear read of what is in place, who it serves, and what has been waiting: network, Wi-Fi, endpoints, cloud, and the gaps that are actually blocking the work. You leave with what to keep, what to retire, and a sequence you can explain.

Do you work with schools on E-Rate?

Ask us about your district's process — we'll tell you straight whether we fit.

Do you serve both Kansas and Missouri?

Yes. One Day Systems serves the Kansas City metro on both sides of the state line.

Are you a managed IT provider or project-based?

Project-based: evaluate, design, integrate, deploy. We are not a ticket-queue MSP.

How fast can you start?

Often within days of scope agreement. Multi-site school work follows your calendar, including summers and breaks when that is what the buildings need.

What happens after deployment?

Documentation, credentials in your name, training for the people who use it, and a clear handoff.

Who owns what you build?

You can use what we build for your organization for as long as you like. We keep ownership of the underlying designs and tools, so we can keep improving them and reuse them on future projects. You can't resell or hand it off to another company without asking us first. It's all spelled out in our agreement before we start. No surprises.

Do we have to replace everything?

Almost never. We document what you have, keep what still fits, and plan updates ahead of failure, so you only replace what you need, when you need it.
