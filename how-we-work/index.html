How we work

Evaluate. Design. Integrate. Deploy.

One Day Systems is a technology consulting firm serving the Kansas City metro. The work is evaluate, design, integrate, and deploy — IT, AV, communications, security, hardware, and software.

We move quickly. We do not pretend a building, a network, or a company goes live between breakfast and lunch. Speed is a sequence: know the work, decide the design, connect the pieces, put it in service.

  1. 01

    Evaluate

    What is in place, who it serves, and what has been waiting. We talk to the people who feel the gap — not only the person who requested a quote. You get a clear read: what to keep, what to retire, and what is actually blocking the work.

  2. 02

    Design

    A plan you can explain. Scope, sequence, and what good looks like for the operators, the people in the rooms, and whoever has to support it next year. Standards where a standard will be repeated. Specifics where the site is particular.

  3. 03

    Integrate

    This is where separate purchases become one system. Networks, identity, rooms, voice, cameras, applications, and the vendors already in the building. We stay in the middle so the pieces agree.

  4. 04

    Deploy

    Put it in. Prove it with the people who will use it. Leave it supportable — documented, owned, and not dependent on a single memory. Quickly, on a timeline that respects the operation.

How we show up

Small team, agile, ready.

You work directly with the people doing the work. We move fast, change course when we need to, and don’t hand you off to a ticket queue.

Straight answers.

If we’re not the right fit, we’ll tell you. Usually we know someone who is, and we’ll send you their way.

Deterministic when it makes sense, agentic when it helps.

Deterministic is the big word for predictable systems with no AI: same input, same result, every time. That’s the right call for alerts, room control, and anything safety-critical. When AI agents can save real time, like triaging, summarizing, or automating messy workflows, we use them on purpose and put guardrails around them.

Forward-thinking tech that just works.

We like new technology. We like it more once it’s reliable enough that nobody has to think about it.

Buy it however works for you.

We can procure equipment through ODS, through the partners you already use, or point you to the best place to buy it online. Our job is to help you find the best fit, not push a SKU.

Install it however works for you.

Our team, your team, or one of our trusted partners can handle implementation. We’ll plan the work so the handoff is clean whichever way you go.

The name is the promise that the work will not sit on the list forever. It is not a claim that a project finishes overnight, or that anything is “installed in a day.”
