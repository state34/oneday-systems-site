One Day Systems is a technology consulting firm serving the Kansas City metro that designs and builds custom software and system integrations for schools, businesses, government, and industrial sites.

The problem

A spreadsheet is doing a job a product should do. People retype between systems. The internal tool was scoped once, then life happened, and it joined the list called “one day.”

How we work

Evaluate

The work, not only the feature request. Where time actually goes, and which swivel-chair step should not exist.

Design

A tool, an integration, or an automation the people who live in it will recognize. Small enough to finish. Clear enough to explain.

Integrate

Connected to the systems you already run, so the new piece removes a step instead of adding a login.

Deploy

In people’s hands, with a handover. Not a repository and a hope.

Typical outcomes

  • Custom tools that earn a place in the week.
  • Integrations that retire the retyping.

Questions

Do you build custom software or only connect systems we already have?

Both, depending on the work. Custom tools when a workaround should retire. Integrations when the systems you already run should finally talk to each other.

Will the software only run if you stay involved?

No. Deployment includes documentation, credentials in your name, and a handoff to the people who will run it.

Where do you use AI?

When it saves real time, such as triaging or summarizing, and with guardrails. Alerts, room control, and anything safety-critical stay predictable: same input, same result.

Are you a managed IT provider or project-based?

Project-based: evaluate, design, integrate, deploy. We are not a ticket-queue MSP.

How fast can you start?

Often within days of scope agreement. Multi-site school work follows your calendar, including summers and breaks when that is what the buildings need.

What happens after deployment?

Documentation, credentials in your name, training for the people who use it, and a clear handoff.

Who owns what you build?

You can use what we build for your organization for as long as you like. We keep ownership of the underlying designs and tools, so we can keep improving them and reuse them on future projects. You can't resell or hand it off to another company without asking us first. It's all spelled out in our agreement before we start. No surprises.

Do we have to replace everything?

Almost never. We document what you have, keep what still fits, and plan updates ahead of failure, so you only replace what you need, when you need it.
