Communications

Business communications and VoIP in Kansas City

Communications that match how people talk

One Day Systems is a technology consulting firm serving the Kansas City metro that evaluates, designs, integrates and deploys business phone, VoIP, and paging systems for organizations.

The problem

The phone system, the meeting app, and the rooms each have their own logic. Joining a call has become a skill. Connectivity shows up as the first item on the agenda instead of the thing nobody notices.

How we work

Evaluate

The paths people really use — desk, mobile, room, and field — not only the diagram from the last carrier.

Design

One collaboration story. Voice, meetings, and the network agree on how a person reaches another person.

Integrate

Carriers, apps, and rooms connected so a meeting is a meeting, whether someone is at a desk or down the hall.

Deploy

Rolled out so the first week is not a scavenger hunt. People get a path they will actually use.

Typical outcomes

  • Calls that reach a person.
  • Rooms that join the meeting without a rehearsal.
  • Connectivity that stays off the agenda.

Questions

Can you replace a phone system without a long outage?

We plan the cut so people can still reach the organization. The sequence is agreed before anything is unplugged, and we prove the new system with the people who use it.

Do you handle paging as well as desk phones?

Yes. Phone, VoIP, paging, and collaboration, including alerts that have to reach a room, a screen, or a phone.

Which brands do you install?

We select against the design. We are not locked to one manufacturer.

Do you serve both Kansas and Missouri?

Yes. One Day Systems serves the Kansas City metro on both sides of the state line.

How fast can you start?

Often within days of scope agreement. Multi-site school work follows your calendar, including summers and breaks when that is what the buildings need.

What happens after deployment?

Documentation, credentials in your name, training for the people who use it, and a clear handoff.

Who owns what you build?

You can use what we build for your organization for as long as you like. We keep ownership of the underlying designs and tools, so we can keep improving them and reuse them on future projects. You can't resell or hand it off to another company without asking us first. It's all spelled out in our agreement before we start. No surprises.

Do we have to replace everything?

Almost never. We document what you have, keep what still fits, and plan updates ahead of failure, so you only replace what you need, when you need it.
