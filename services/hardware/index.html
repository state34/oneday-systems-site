Hardware chosen for the work

The problem

Gear gets bought in a hurry, quoted into a stall, or left unsupported six months later. The wrong display, a switch that cannot carry the room, laptops that never joined the standard. Good design dies in the purchase.

How we work

Evaluate

What you already own, what is failing the work, and what a quote is pretending to solve.

Design

A standard: the devices that fit the rooms, the desks, and the network — specific enough to reorder.

Integrate

Installed so the hardware belongs to the system. Labeled, recorded, and talking to the rest of it.

Deploy

In service, with support that already knows what is on the wall. Not a pile of boxes and a goodbye.

Typical outcomes

  • Devices that fit the work.
  • A record of what you own, and a standard you can buy again.
  • Support from people who already know the design.
